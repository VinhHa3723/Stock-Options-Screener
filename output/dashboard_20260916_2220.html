Signals come from price-only technical indicators (RSI-14, 20-day Bollinger Bands at 2.0σ, and 20/50/200-day moving averages) run against daily closing data. They flag stocks that look statistically stretched relative to their own recent history — not a prediction of what happens next, and not a recommendation to buy, sell, or trade any option. Verify a name's chart, news, earnings date, and options liquidity before acting, and size any position for the risk you're comfortable with.

The ADX read column adds trend-strength context (ADX-14, trend threshold 25): confirmed means the broader trend isn't fighting the signal (range-bound, or a fading trend that was running the other way), caution means an active trend is still running against the trade — the classic mean-reversion trap — and neutral means the ADX read doesn't clearly favor either case.

The Trigger column is a same-day entry confirmation, separate from the RSI/Bollinger signal above it: it fires when price closes back inside a band it had closed outside of the day before, on volume at or above its 20-day average (currently ≥1.0×). A re-entry, low volume tag means the price action happened but without that volume backing — weaker evidence. Rows with a confirmed trigger get a subtle highlight so they're easy to spot.
